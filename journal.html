We started with a simple irritation: useful things keep becoming trash because nobody can see the next move in time.

CHAOS · FIELD NOTESCOMPASS · BEARINGBUILDING IN PUBLIC

Hi. I'm Chaos. I did not set out to build a recycling company. I got irritated that people were paying to throw away things that still had value.

I was not trying to save the world. I wanted to make a buck. Then the deeper I dug, the clearer something became: the money was not hiding inside the plastic, the copper, or the old equipment.

It was hiding inside organization.

The signal arrives late.

A resident has a pile. A store has clean cardboard. A contractor has a swap-out coming. A dentist has equipment leaving service. The value may be real, but the signal arrives late—or never.

By the time the right person learns about it, the material is mixed, damaged, hauled away, or impossible to trace.

CHAOS
What if every useful thing had a memory before it left?
COMPASS
Then we would need identity, evidence, custody, a destination, and proof of what happened next.

The dragon's hoard.

FIELD METAPHOR

Accumulation is not the same as value. A collection can become so dense, expensive, and difficult to understand that the treasure begins to own the dragon.

The same thing happens in garages, warehouses, businesses, scrapyards, archives, and databases. Useful things are everywhere. Their relationships are missing.

Watchtower is our attempt to catch those relationships early enough to make a better next move.

What works today.

The resident demo can use a phone camera or photo, identify a material, create a device-local demo asset, and walk it through a clearly simulated lifecycle. It does not create money, a token, a sale, or a payment obligation.

Everything beyond that has to be earned through real partners, verified operations, and evidence. We will keep a bright line between the network we can imagine and the network we can prove.

COMPASS · HERE'S OUR BEARING

Chaos brings experience. Compass brings evidence. Neither is complete without the other. The purpose is not to win arguments, but to uncover truth that helps people create more value.

This is Field Journal #000: the first marker on the map. We are building in public, starting in Ocala, and preserving the wrong turns along with the breakthroughs.
