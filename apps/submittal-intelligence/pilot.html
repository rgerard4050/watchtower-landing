Frequently asked questions.

Does Morrow approve the submittal?

No. The founding pilot is a human-reviewed preflight. The contractor, GC, architect, engineer, owner, and code officials retain their respective review responsibilities.

Does Morrow rewrite manufacturer product data?

No. Source documents remain authentic. Morrow recommends corrections, replacement selections, missing documents, or a properly identified deviation request.

How are the two PDFs handled?

After a free pilot request is accepted, the customer emails one authorized specification and one proposed submittal for the human-reviewed pilot. Do not send classified, export-controlled, privileged, prohibited, or unnecessary personal data. The self-service AI upload remains a separate demonstration until a production analysis provider is funded and approved.

What if the package needs professional judgment?

Morrow labels uncertainty and routes final decisions to the responsible contractor and design professional. It does not make engineering, safety, code, or approval determinations.
